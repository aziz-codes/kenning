# Flag it, and the agent fixes it

Spot something wrong on a card? Press **F**, say what's wrong, and send it.

- **While Claude is working,** it gets your note before it finishes and addresses it straight away.
- **If Claude is idle,** the note arrives with your next message.
- **With other agents,** Kenning copies a ready-to-paste note to your clipboard.

Flagging counts toward your understanding score. Spotting a problem means you read the change.
