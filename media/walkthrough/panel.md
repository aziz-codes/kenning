# The full review

The sidebar is made for quick checks while the agent works. When a change needs a proper look, open the review in an editor tab: click **⤢** on a card, press **E**, or run **Kenning: Open full review**.

- **Every changed file** is listed on the left, riskiest changes first.
- **Side by side or unified.** Press **V** to switch.
- **Whole file.** Press **W** to see the change in the context of its entire file. The change is highlighted, with the rest of the file around it.
- **History.** Press **H** for your understanding score and reviews over the last 30 days.
- **Copy review summary** gives you a Markdown summary of what you reviewed and flagged, ready for a pull request.
